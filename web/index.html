    About the data

    Shaking: USGS ShakeMap for the HayWired M7.05 scenario. Its cells are about 1.5 by 1.85 km, and it doesn't model local soil effects, so shaking here doesn't separate bay fill from bedrock.

    Risk score: an index of shaking, ground failure, building vulnerability and retrofit status. It ranks buildings. It isn't a damage estimate.

    Sources: DataSF (soft-story list, assessor roll, footprints, parcels, neighborhoods), USGS (HayWired, faults), California Geological Survey, US Census. Basemap © OpenFreeMap, © OpenStreetMap contributors.
